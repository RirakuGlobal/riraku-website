The character of Japan's regions. Truly delicious food. Delivered to the ends of the world.

Riraku Co., Ltd. is a Tokyo-based trading company that brings Japanese food products to Asia, East Africa and North America. We handle both exports and imports, connecting producers with the people who enjoy their food.

Talk to us about trade

Business

Exporting Japanese food

We export food products from Japanese manufacturers to markets in Asia, East Africa and North America, and support them in working with local importers and wholesalers.

Importing food to Japan

We import food products from Taiwan, Kenya and other countries, and introduce them to buyers in Japan.

Food and beverage consulting

We advise on overseas expansion, product development and other topics related to the food and beverage business.
